FAQ

Every question answered.

Will it conflict with Yoast SEO or RankMath?

No. God Mode operates on a separate AEO layer and detects existing schema plugins automatically. It adds AI-specific optimisations without overwriting your existing SEO setup.

Does it work with my page builder?

Yes — tested with Elementor, Bricks, Divi, Oxygen, Kadence, and GeneratePress. God Mode reads your content, not your builder's markup, so compatibility is universal.

Do I need an API key or account?

No. God Mode runs entirely on your server with no external API calls required. Install, activate, done. Your data never leaves your WordPress install.

How does the competitive gap analysis work?

God Mode monitors AI-answer patterns in your content niche, identifies queries where competitors are being cited, then generates a specific fix list for your pages to displace them.

What counts as an AI citation?

Any time your URL is referenced as a source in an AI answer — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The dashboard tracks each citation with the query and engine.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Free forever under GPL v2. The full plugin — schema detection, llms.txt, crawler control, audit, and gap analysis — is completely free. There is no freemium upsell wall.

How long before I see results?

Most sites see AI crawlers respecting new policies within 48 hours. Citation improvements typically appear in 2–6 weeks as engines re-index your structured content.

Does it work on WooCommerce product pages?

Yes. God Mode auto-detects Product schema for WooCommerce, adding price, availability, and review structured data — optimised for AI shopping queries.
